# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
showing empathy, being respectful of differing opinions, and accepting
constructive criticism.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
personal attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through the private
contact method listed in the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will
be reviewed and handled confidentially when possible.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
